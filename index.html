Senior Researcher at Tencent Games · LightSpeed Studios
Ph.D. from HKUST (2025), advised by Prof. Lei Chen

My research vision is to make AI systems reason reliably over data. Rooted in data management, I develop retrieval-augmented generation and agent memory that turn raw data and knowledge into trustworthy LLM and agent behavior — from question answering to scientific discovery.

Research Research Interests

I build data-centric methods and benchmarks for language models that must retrieve, remember, and reason over structured and unstructured knowledge.

Reliable Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Systems and benchmarks for grounding LLM behavior in external data, knowledge graphs, and verifiable retrieval pipelines.

Agent Memory and Long-Horizon Reasoning

Memory structures, freshness checks, and anchoring mechanisms for agents operating across extended conversations and tasks.

Data Management for AI Systems

Methods for semantic typing, text-to-SQL, taxonomy reasoning, and scientific data interfaces that connect models with real data.
